Rescan devices
Layout
To get your own title drawn in the key's style, hide Stream Deck's title with the "T" toggle next to the Title field. Stream Deck paints its title on top of the plugin's image and won't let a plugin clear a title you typed, so while it is visible the plugin leaves the name line to the device name instead of printing the same text twice. Stream Deck ignores plugin titles on a key where you typed your own title — clear the Title field above to let this take effect.
Thresholds
Alert below 0 is off. Above 0, the key flashes Stream Deck's warning icon on every refresh while the level is under it.
Colours
While charging the key shows a bolt and breathes slowly in the charging colour.
About
Detected via HeadsetControl (headsets), HID++ (Logitech), USB descriptors (Asus) and the Windows Bluetooth battery property. Devices marked "no battery data" were found but expose no readable battery level.
