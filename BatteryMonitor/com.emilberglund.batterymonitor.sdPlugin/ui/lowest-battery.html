About what counts

Peripherals only leaves out phones, tablets and watches. They have their own chargers and their own warnings, and counting them means the key spends its time reporting a phone instead of the headset that's about to go quiet mid-call.

Only live readings count. A device that's switched off can't win with the level it had yesterday, and a mains-powered one has nothing to compare.

No extra scanning happens for this: every device's level is already collected, so this only chooses between readings that exist.
