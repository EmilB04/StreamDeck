About renaming

Names come from the operating system or from the device's own descriptor, and some of them are useless — Windows reports one phone here as 4, and unbranded Bluetooth gear often arrives as "Bluetooth device". Where that name can't be fixed at the source, fix it here.

A key's Nickname does the same thing for one key and takes priority over this. Use a rename when the device should be called the same thing everywhere, and a nickname when one key wants its own wording.

Names are matched to the device's stable key, not its position in the list, so they survive reconnects, reboots and the device being switched off.
